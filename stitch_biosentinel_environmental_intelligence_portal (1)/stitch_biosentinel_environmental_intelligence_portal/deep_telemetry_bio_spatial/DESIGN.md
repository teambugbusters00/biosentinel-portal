## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an operational aesthetic positioned at the intersection of a mission-critical federal environmental observatory, a tactical geospatial telemetry cockpit, and an advanced automated machine-intelligence platform. Designed primarily for mobile-first deployments in the field and operational response units, the visual tone conveys institutional authority, scientific calibration, and situational urgency without visual noise.

The design movement combines **Modern Tactical Minimalism** with **Atmospheric GIS Glassmorphism**:
- **Field Utility & Precision:** Clean technical layouts prioritizing tabular telemetry, high-legibility spatial readouts, and immediate threat identification over ornamental styling.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep-water aquatic undertones (deep abyssal teals and rich dark slates) layered with translucent glass containers, high-contrast bioluminescent accents, and finely etched geometric borders.
- **Mission Readiness:** High visual rigor, calibrated sensor chips, confidence intervals, and verifiable institutional credentialing designed to perform under harsh outdoor daylight and night-mode field monitoring.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for high-contrast environmental telemetry and spatial overlays in a native dark environment.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Foundation Base (`#0B1924`):** Abyssal navy-teal baseline used for primary screen backgrounds, root mobile canvas, and underlying map viewports.
- **Surface Subtle (`#122330`):** Rich riverbed slate for nested groupings, list backdrops, and secondary section segmentations.
- **Surface Default (`#1B2F3F`):** Calibrated surface tier for interactive cards, sheets, telemetry widgets, and floating map drawer controls.
- **Surface Elevated (`#1E384D`):** Elevated tactile surface used for active overlays, modals, high-priority popovers, and sticky bottom navigation bars.

### Telemetry Accents & Bioluminescence
- **Primary Bio-Emerald (`#10B981`):** Bioluminescent emerald representing ecological equilibrium, thriving biodiversity indices, verified detections, and primary interactive affordances.
- **Secondary Bio-Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Hydrological telemetry accent reserved for river flow volume, dissolved oxygen meters, water quality indicators (WQI), and active GIS vectors.
- **Tertiary Certification Gold (`#EAB308`):** National mission verification badges, certified sensor hardware seals, and peer-reviewed telemetry validations.
- **River Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Threshold alerts, seasonal anomalies, moderate discharge warnings, and degrading canopy signals.
- **Incident Critical Red (`#EF4444`):** Critical ecological breach, toxic run-off anomalies, illegal extraction warnings, and real-time telemetry sensor dropouts.

### Typography & Structure
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp stark white ensuring AAA contrast on dark containers for headline readings, metrics, and primary status text.
- **Text Secondary & Neutral Titanium (`#94A3B8`):** Cool slate-gray for tabular coordinate labels, unit denominations, inactive states, and structural dividers.

## Typography

The typography architecture uses a functional division between narrative metadata and scientific instrumentation.

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its structural geometric clarity and contemporary authority. It scales gracefully on mobile displays while retaining a decisive posture suitable for national infrastructure and environmental intelligence reports.
- **Narrative & System Interface (`Inter`):** Used across functional UI strings, incident narratives, and descriptions. Provides neutral, undistorted reading efficiency at small body scales.
- **Instrumentation & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Strictly enforced for numeric streams, tabular arrays, GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude), confidence percentages, pH balances, and sensor node identifiers. Monospaced character tracking prevents optical layout jitter as live telemetry updates in real-time.
- **Micro Labels & Badges:** All telemetry classification tags leverage `label-caps` in uppercase styling with extended letter spacing (`0.08em`) to guarantee quick recognition in high-glare field environments.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a fluid, high-density rhythm designed for touch-screen field ergonomics. 

### Grid & Ergonomics
- **Base Grid:** 4px geometric scaling base unit (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-base` = 16px).
- **Mobile Edge Boundaries:** Enforced `16px` (`margin-mobile`) horizontal safe margin on all mobile viewports, expanding to `24px` on tablets.
- **48px Tactical Target Rules:** All actionable elements (telemetry chips, record buttons, GPS layer toggles) maintain a minimum hit boundary of 48×48px, even when visual surface representation is compact (e.g., 32px pill).
- **Z-Axis Spatial Pinning:** Map layers render underneath persistent UI. Top telemetry app-bar sits at `appbar-height` (56px) with an edge-anchored telemetry HUD below it. Primary action hubs (citizen upload trigger and stream selector) sit anchored above the sticky bottom navigation dock (`dock-height` of 72px).
- **Responsive Adaptations:** Single-column vertical stream on mobile screens (`<600px`). Above 600px, GIS split views activate, allocating 60% viewport width to the spatial map engine and 40% to telemetry diagnostic cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy utilizes low-reflectance tonal layering, soft geospatial glows, and tactile micro-borders rather than opaque, disconnected drop shadows.

- **Tier 0 (Map Baseline):** Pure `#0B1924` abyssal canvas or live satellite imagery. No elevation.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Canvas & Panels):** `#122330` with a 1px structural boundary of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Telemetry Cards):** `#1B2F3F` equipped with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background alpha blending (`rgba(27, 47, 63, 0.82)`), and an inner highlight keyline `border-top: 1px solid rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.12)`. Ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(3, 10, 16, 0.6)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating HUD Controls & Alerts):** `#1E384D` elevated with `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(2, 6, 10, 0.8)`. Bounded by active telemetry state borders:
  - Nominal: `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`
  - Alert/Breach: `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.5)` with an ambient bloom `0 0 16px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)`
- **Tier 4 (Critical Incident Modals & Drawers):** `#1E384D` solid backing over `rgba(11, 25, 36, 0.85)` backdrop dimming filter.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (`1`)** shape geometry, pairing precision-machined technical corners with purposeful pill shapes for status tags.

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius (`rounded-lg`). Retains an instrumentation-grade hardware silhouette.
- **Telemetry Readouts & Input Fields:** Uniform `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius to align with monospaced data alignment.
- **Status Pills & Telemetry Nodes:** Full capsule/pill styling (`9999px`) reserved exclusively for dynamic states (e.g., "AI CONFIDENCE: 98.4%", "ACTIVE FLOW", "SENSOR CALIBRATING") to create an instant cognitive distinction between interactive modules and informational status nodes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Mission Button:** Background `#10B981`, label in `#0B1924` (Bold 600). 48px height, 8px border radius. Hover/Active transforms surface to `#059669` with an emerald telemetry glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 14px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Telemetry Button:** Background `#1B2F3F`, border `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`, text `#06B6D4`. Provides clear spatial context without overpowering the primary trigger.
- **Tactical Field Capture Button (FAB):** Circular 56px action unit anchored bottom-right. Ring-illuminated outer border (`2px solid #10B981`), surface `#122330`, housing camera/sensor capture icons in stark `#F8FAFC`.

### Chips & Basin Status Badges
- **Interactive Basin Filter Chips:** Height 32px, radius 9999px. Inactive state: `#122330` background, `1px solid #1B2F3F`, text `#94A3B8`. Active state: `#1B2F3F` background, `1px solid #06B6D4`, text `#F8FAFC`, paired with an active pulsing status dot.
- **Verification Authority Badge:** Compact pill featuring `#EAB308` border, micro-gold institutional seal icon, and monospaced text "NATL-ENV-VERIFIED".

### Cards & GIS Data Modules
- **Water Quality Index (WQI) Dial Card:** Glass-morphic backing (`#1B2F3F` at 85% opacity), displaying high-contrast monospaced numeral readout (`28px`), circular gauge ring colored dynamically according to WQI scale, and localized coordinate subtext.
- **Species Confidence Card:** Displays high-resolution specimen imagery accompanied by an AI bounding matrix, an authoritative species taxonomy title in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, and an inline confidence indicator: `JetBrains Mono` badge `[CONF: 96.2%]` tinted in Bio-Emerald.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Tactical Field Inputs:** Background `#122330`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. On focus: border shifts to `#06B6D4` with zero drop shadow, replaced instead by a 1px sharp outer ring.
- **Telemetry Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp squircle shape (2px radius). Unchecked: hollow slate ring. Checked: solid `#10B981` fill with an inset dark checkmark.

### Telemetry Meters & Lists
- **Telemetry List Item:** Divided by `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`. Layout pairs scientific label (left) with high-density tabular readout and dynamic variance delta (`+1.4%` in Bio-Cyan or `-4.2%` in Critical Red) on the right.
